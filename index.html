01 / ABOUT ME

A little about me.

I don't just want a successful life. I want an interesting one.

I'm Lucas. I love being active, travelling, discovering films that stay with me, and spending time with people who make life better.

I'm ambitious, but I don't want ambition to become my entire personality. I want to take opportunities, challenge myself, make memories and enjoy the process.

My Christian faith is an important part of who I am. I value purpose, gratitude, meaningful relationships and trying to become a better person.
